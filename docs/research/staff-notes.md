# What shop staff look up

Notes from talking to the staff at Larkspur Ceramics and Hollow Oak Candles about what they use Stockroom for, and what takes them longest. October 2026.

## Tom, support at Larkspur

- Most of his day is customer emails. Nearly every one quotes an order number: "Has order 1154 shipped?", "Where's my parcel?" He opens the order, reads the status and the dates, and writes back.
- "What else has this customer bought from us?" when someone's a regular or is complaining.
- Refunds for things that arrive broken, about five a week. Usually one item, sometimes the whole order. He can refund up to £50; anything bigger goes to Nadia.
- He'd like to "just tell it to email the customer an update". Stockroom doesn't send emails today.

## Priya, warehouse at Larkspur

- First thing every morning: "Which orders are waiting to be packed?", and which of those are late.
- "Which products are running low?" so she can tell Nadia what to reorder.
- She packs and posts, so she sees delivery addresses. She doesn't need customer emails and doesn't do refunds.

## Nadia, owner at Larkspur

- "How many orders did we take last month?" and how this week is going.
- Wants to change prices and add products faster. Today she does that on the Products page.
- Worried about two things: an assistant doing something nobody asked for, and the bill.

## Owen, owner at Hollow Oak

- Much the same questions as Nadia, for his shop.
- Asked whether "the AI" could ever see Larkspur's orders. Both shops are on the same Stockroom.
